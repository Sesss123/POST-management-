# Cashier Training Manual - RestoLedger POS

Welcome to RestoLedger! This guide covers the essential workflows for cashiers and billing staff.

## 1. Daily Opening
1. Log in with your cashier credentials.
2. Go to **Shift Management**.
3. Open a new shift by recording the **Opening Cash** in the drawer.

## 2. Quick Retail (Over-the-Counter)
1. Select items from the menu.
2. Click **Pay Now**.
3. Select payment method (Cash, Card, QR).
4. For cash: enter amount received and give change.
5. Provide the thermal receipt to the customer.

## 3. Table Billing (Dine-In)
1. **Open Table**: Click an available table (Green), select waiter, and click **Open Session**.
2. **Add Items**: Select items and click **Send to Kitchen** to generate KOT.
3. **Bill Request**: When the customer asks for the bill, click **Print Bill**.
4. **Checkout**: Click **Checkout**, apply any discounts, and process payment. The table will turn Green again.

## 4. Naya Book (Credit Sales)
1. Only available for registered customers with a credit limit.
2. During checkout, select **Credit (Naya)** as the payment method.
3. Search for the customer name.
4. If they have sufficient limit, the sale will be recorded in their ledger.

## 5. Held Bills (Parking)
1. If a customer needs to step away, click **Hold Bill**.
2. To resume, go to **Held Bills** and select the parked transaction.

## 6. Daily Closing
1. At the end of your shift, go to **Shift Management**.
2. Record the **Closing Cash** in the drawer.
3. Print the **Shift End Report** to reconcile with the system totals.
4. Click **Close Shift**.

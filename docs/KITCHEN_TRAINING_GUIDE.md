# Kitchen Display System (KDS) Manual - RestoLedger POS

This guide covers how to use the KDS to manage orders and preparation times.

## 1. Viewing Orders
1. New orders appear as cards in the **New** column.
2. Orders are sorted by time (Oldest first).
3. Red cards indicate high-priority orders that have exceeded preparation targets.

## 2. Order Details
- **Item Name & Quantity**: Large font for readability.
- **Modifiers**: Highlights extra requests (e.g., "Extra Spicy", "No Onion").
- **Table Number**: Identified at the top of the card.
- **Timer**: Shows how long the order has been waiting.

## 3. Workflow Transitions
1. **Start Cooking**: Click the **Start** button on a card to move it to the **Preparing** column.
2. **Complete Order**: When the dish is ready, click **Ready** to move it to the **Completed** column.
3. **Notify Server**: Completing an order will automatically notify the waiter on their POS view.

## 4. Item Availability
1. If an item is out of stock, go to **Items** and toggle the **Sold Out** status.
2. This will immediately prevent cashiers from selling that item until it is toggled back to **Available**.

## 5. View Filters
- Use the **Category Filter** to show only relevant sections (e.g., "Bar" vs "Kitchen").
- Toggle **Sound Alerts** to be notified of new incoming orders.
